# Your Life in Weeks

A free, interactive life calendar that visualizes your entire lifespan as a constellation of stars — each one a single week, month, or year of your life.

Enter your birthday and watch the weeks you've lived ignite, colored by life stage, against the weeks still ahead.

**Live site:** https://life-in-weeks-green.vercel.app/

## Features
- Switch between Weeks, Months, and Years views
- Animated row-by-row star ignition
- Life-stage color coding
- Cursor-reactive glowing stars and click ripples
- Hover any star to see its exact week, age, and year
- "Week of the Year" and "Age" axis guides
- Download your grid as an image to share
- FAQ section with structured data for SEO
- Fully responsive — works on mobile
- No sign-up, no tracking — all calculation happens in your browser

## Built with
Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single self-contained `index.html`. No build step, no dependencies, no frameworks.

## Deployment
Hosted on Vercel, auto-deploying from this repository's `main` branch.
